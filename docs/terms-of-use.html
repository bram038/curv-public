Curv Terms of Use

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Curv is provided for route planning, community route discovery, and navigation assistance. By using Curv, you agree to use the app responsibly and follow applicable traffic laws and road conditions.

Accounts and Community Routes

Curv accounts are used to link saved routes, route history, published routes, likes, Premium access, and subscription recovery to a stable user identity.

You are responsible for the routes and content you publish. Do not publish routes or content that is unlawful, harmful, misleading, abusive, or infringes the rights of others.

Navigation and Safety

Curv may provide route and navigation guidance, but it is not a substitute for your own judgment.

You are responsible for your safety and must follow traffic laws, posted signs, access restrictions, and current road conditions. Do not use Curv in a way that distracts you or creates unsafe riding conditions.

Curv can be used with Apple CarPlay and other in-vehicle displays. When using Curv this way, only interact with it when it is safe to do so, keep your attention on the road, and do not let on-screen guidance override what you see around you. CarPlay is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's own terms.

Curv Premium

Curv Premium is available as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Premium access is linked to your Curv account for saved routes, community activity, route history, and subscription recovery.

Account Deletion

You can delete your Curv account from inside the app.

Deleting your Curv account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. You must manage or cancel subscriptions separately in your Apple ID account settings.
